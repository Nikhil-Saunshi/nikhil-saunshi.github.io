About

I'm an automation engineer first. For years at Meta and Apple I've built the Python frameworks that decide whether hardware is allowed to ship, validating GPU clusters, embedded platforms, and battery systems at fleet scale. Networking, firmware, OS, silicon: those are the layers my automation interrogates, not my job title. And every verdict my frameworks deliver is data-driven, built on measurements, baselines, and regression evidence rather than gut feel.

In 2025 I put LLM agents inside a production validation framework, orchestrating 500+ test procedures back when "AI in the test loop" was a slide rather than a practice. It cut cycle time by 35%. Off the clock I ship complete systems: an algorithmic trading framework with walk-forward validation gates, and a B2B pharma platform serving medical shops across India. Published at IEEE BigData along the way.
